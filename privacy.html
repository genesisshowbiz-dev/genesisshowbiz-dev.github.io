Privacy Policy

How this website may collect, use and protect information provided by visitors.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated for The Genesis DNA Reality Show, a Kenya-based media and sponsored DNA-testing programme. You can contact us at genesis.show.biz@gmail.com or +254 731 556 341.

2. Information you provide

If you contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email or another communication channel, you may provide information such as your name, contact details, family circumstances, case information or documents. Sensitive case information should only be shared when necessary and through the contact process directed by the Genesis team.

3. Website and advertising data

This website may use services provided by third parties, including Google AdSense, that may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalise advertising where permitted. Google and its partners may process data in accordance with their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and assess programme requests.
  • To communicate about appointments, documentation or participation.
  • To operate and improve the website and programme.
  • To meet legal, safety, record-keeping and production obligations where applicable.

5. DNA, health and family information

DNA results, family relationships and identity information are highly sensitive. This public website is not intended as a portal for uploading DNA reports or confidential records. Where documents are required for a case, the Genesis team will explain the appropriate process.

6. Media participation

Participation in filmed programme content is governed by separate consent, appearance and release documents. Those documents, rather than this website policy, describe the permissions associated with recording, editing and publishing programme content.

7. Information sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or professionals involved in testing, production, administration or legal compliance when necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not publish private case information merely because someone has contacted the programme.

8. Data retention and security

We aim to keep information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legal, contractual, production and record-keeping requirements. No online or electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

9. Your choices

You may contact us to ask questions about information you have provided through this website or our general enquiry channels. Rights and obligations may vary depending on the nature of the information and applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website, advertising services or programme processes change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

This website policy is a general operational notice and is not a substitute for the specific consent and release documents used for programme participation.
