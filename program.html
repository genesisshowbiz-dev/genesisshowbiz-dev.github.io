The Free DNA Programme

Designed to help qualifying families access paternity testing when the normal cost of testing may be out of reach. Participation is subject to eligibility, documentation, consent and programme requirements.

Who the programme is for

The programme supports families facing genuine paternity uncertainty and seeking scientific clarification. The alleged father and mother must generally be adults aged 18 or older and able to present valid identification and relevant supporting documents.

Important: Sponsored testing does not mean every enquiry automatically qualifies. Cases are reviewed individually, and participation depends on programme capacity, documentation, consent and testing requirements.

Core requirements

  • Alleged father and/or mother must be 18+ and provide valid identification.
  • Supporting documentation should be provided where relevant.
  • For a child, a birth notification or birth certificate should be provided where available.
  • All required parties must attend the scheduled collection or production process.
  • Participants sign legally binding consent and appearance/release forms before participation.
  • No food or other substances should be taken for 30 minutes before sample collection, in line with the collection instructions provided.
  • A KSh 2,000 non-refundable registration / consultation fee applies.

When a parent is unavailable

In some circumstances, kinship testing may be considered when a parent is unavailable. The appropriate relatives and testing route depend on the biological relationship, the available participants and the laboratory’s requirements. The Genesis team will explain whether a kinship pathway may be practical for a particular case.

Recording and publication

The Genesis DNA Reality Show is a media programme as well as a sponsored testing initiative. Participating cases may be filmed, recorded and published on Genesis social platforms. Participants must understand and sign the relevant consent and media-release documents before production.

Children and vulnerable family members

Cases involving children require particular care. A DNA result may answer a biological question, but adults remain responsible for handling the result responsibly and protecting the child from unnecessary conflict, humiliation or exposure. Where a legal or welfare issue exists, families should seek appropriate professional advice.

What the programme does not promise

Acceptance into the programme does not guarantee a particular DNA result, family outcome, legal outcome, financial support arrangement or relationship outcome. The scientific result speaks to biological relationship; other decisions may require legal, medical, counselling or social-support professionals.

  • ✓Valid ID for adult participants
  • ✓Child birth documentation where available
  • ✓A clear summary of the paternity concern
  • ✓Availability of all required participants
