Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you participate.

These answers explain the programme at a general level. Individual cases may require additional information or different testing arrangements.

Is DNA testing completely free through the Genesis programme?

The testing itself is sponsored for qualifying programme participants. A KSh 2,000 non-refundable registration / consultation fee applies.

Does every applicant qualify?

No. Cases are reviewed individually. Eligibility depends on documentation, participant availability, consent, programme capacity and whether the case can be handled within the programme’s testing and production requirements.

What identification do adults need?

Adult participants should have valid identification and any relevant supporting documents requested by the Genesis team.

What documents are needed for a child?

A birth notification or birth certificate should be provided where available, together with any other documents requested for the specific case.

Must all participants attend?

All required participants must attend the scheduled process. The exact people required depend on whether the case is a direct paternity test or a kinship test.

Can relatives be tested if a parent is unavailable?

Sometimes. Kinship testing may be considered when the necessary parent is unavailable. The suitability of the test depends on which biological relatives can participate.

Why is there a 30-minute food and substance restriction?

Participants are asked to avoid food and other substances for the period specified before oral sample collection to support clean sample collection. Follow the exact instructions given for your appointment.

Will my case be recorded?

The Genesis DNA Reality Show is a filmed media programme. Participating cases may be recorded and published across Genesis platforms. Participants sign consent and media-release documents before production.

Can I participate without consenting to publication?

The sponsored programme is linked to the Genesis media format, so publication consent is part of participation. Ask the team about the specific terms before signing any document.

Does a DNA result decide legal fatherhood or child maintenance?

A DNA result is biological evidence. Legal questions such as parental responsibility, custody, maintenance, guardianship or inheritance may require separate legal advice or formal procedures.
